About Us

Nguru Best Care Enterprises (NBCE) is a leading waste management company dedicated to creating environmental and public health solutions in Yobe State. Our work integrates climate change mitigation, recycling innovation, public health protection, and sustainable community development.

From waste segregation and recycling to eco-friendly landfill alternatives and smart waste systems, our mission is to make Yobe State cleaner, greener, and more climate resilient.

Our Climate-Friendly Services

Waste Collection

Community-focused and environmentally sound waste collection for homes, markets, and institutions.

Recycling & Upcycling

Transforming waste into useful products while reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

Climate Change Awareness

Educating communities on climate-smart behaviors and sustainable waste practices.

Eco-Friendly Composting

Hazardous Waste Management

Proper disposal of medical and chemical waste to protect public health.
